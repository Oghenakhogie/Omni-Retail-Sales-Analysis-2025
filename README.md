# Omini-Retail-Sales-Analysis-2025

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the 2025 sales performance of **OmniRetail Africa Ltd.** using Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Power BI.

The objective was to transform sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides management with a clear understanding of the company's performance across different months, quarters, product categories, countries, branches and customer generations.

The dashboard presents monthly, quarterly and annual performance reports, allowing users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify revenue drivers, evaluate business performance and make informed decisions.

## Business Problems Addressed

The project was designed to address several business challenges that a retail organisation may encounter when managing and evaluating sales performance.

### 1. Limited visibility into overall business performance
Without a consolidated performance report, management may struggle to understand the company's overall sales position.

**Solution:** Developed an annual sales overview displaying key metrics, including:
- Annual Net Sales
- Annual Gross Sales
- Total Transactions
- Total Customers
- Total Units Sold
- Total Discount Amount

### 2. Difficulty identifying revenue drivers
Management needs to understand which products and product categories contribute most to overall revenue.

**Solution:** Developed product category performance visuals to identify high-performing categories and compare their net sales contributions.

**Key finding:** Electronics was the best-performing product category, generating approximately ₦32.30 million in net sales.

### 3. Poor visibility into geographical sales performance
Businesses operating across multiple locations need to identify profitable markets and branches to support resource allocation and expansion decisions.

**Solution:** Analysed net sales across five countries and multiple branches.

**Key findings:**
- Nigeria was the highest-performing country, generating approximately ₦31.87 million.
- Lagos Island was the highest-performing branch, generating approximately ₦20.18 million.

### 4. Difficulty monitoring sales trends and quarterly performance
Without periodic performance monitoring, management may fail to recognise sales fluctuations, growth patterns or declining performance.

**Solution:** Created monthly and quarterly reports to monitor net sales, units sold and quarterly growth rates.

**Key findings:**
- Q1 recorded the highest quarterly net sales at approximately ₦19.32 million.
- Q4 recorded approximately ₦18.88 million.
- Q2 experienced a quarterly decline of approximately 5.24%, while Q3 and Q4 recorded positive growth.

### 5. Limited understanding of customer purchasing patterns
Understanding customer segments can help businesses improve marketing strategies and target their products more effectively.

**Solution:** Analysed sales performance across customer generations, including Baby Boomers, Generation X, Millennials, Generation Z and Unknown.

**Key finding:** Generation X generated the highest net sales contribution, at approximately ₦21.59 million.

### 6. Difficulty evaluating the impact of discounts
Discounts can influence purchasing behaviour, but excessive discounting may reduce the revenue realised from sales.

**Solution:** Incorporated gross sales, net sales and discount amount KPIs to provide visibility into the relationship between sales before and after discounts.

### 7. Limited visibility into operational performance
Sales performance can also be influenced by payment preferences and order fulfilment outcomes.

**Solution:** Incorporated interactive filters for:
- Sales Channel
- Payment Method
- Delivery Status
- Discount Percentage
- Branch
- Customer Generation

These filters allow users to investigate different segments of the business.

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI report is organised into three levels of performance analysis:

### Monthly Sales Analysis
- Daily net sales trends
- Monthly growth rate
- Gross sales versus net sales by country
- Net sales by product category
- Delivery status distribution
- Interactive filtering across business dimensions

### Quarterly Sales Analysis
- Quarterly net sales and gross sales
- Total customers and transactions
- Net sales by branch
- Net sales by month
- Net sales by product category
- Net sales by country
- Net sales by customer generation

### Annual Sales Analysis
- Annual business performance KPIs
- Net sales comparison across quarters
- Quarterly growth rate
- Annual net sales by country
- Annual net sales by branch
- Total units sold by quarter
- Summary of key business performance findings

## Business Value

The project demonstrates how business intelligence can help retail organisations:

- Monitor sales performance across different reporting periods.
- Identify high-performing products, markets and branches.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate the relationship between gross sales, discounts and net sales.
- Identify periods of growth and declining performance.
- Support more informed marketing, inventory and resource allocation decisions.
